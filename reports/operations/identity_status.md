## Defect found and fixed: name order and spelling variants

Three TML players were classified as **new players** before the fix, although the archive knows them: it stores the family name in the first-name field or splits it differently, so the surname index found no candidate. A new canonical ID would have split their histories, and the new-player registry is append-only, so the error would have persisted. Phase 4A identity now also searches names written in another order, and the same birth date with a related name, and holds such players as **ambiguous** (`name_order_or_spelling_variant`). Regression tests: `tests/test_identity_regressions.py`.
