## Verdict

**Statistics compatible: False.** The large differences sit in a few events, notably Grand Slams. The likely cause is different upstream statistics feeds, which the data cannot prove. Grand Slams carry a large share of the serve/return evidence, and excluding the disagreeing events now would be a choice made after seeing the result. TML serve statistics therefore stay **out of the frozen feature engine**: they remain labelled `tml_unverified`, which Phase 4A excludes from statistics. A results-only use is possible once identities are approved: winners agree in 0.9997 of matched matches.
